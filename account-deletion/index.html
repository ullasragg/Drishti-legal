Delete your DRISHTI account

DRISHTI: Tarot & Astrology AI · Android

You can request deletion of your DRISHTI account and its associated data even if you no longer have the app installed.

Request deletion by email

  1. Email trydrishti@gmail.com with the subject DRISHTI account deletion request.
  2. Tell us you want your DRISHTI account and associated data deleted. If you linked Google, use the email address associated with that sign-in if possible. For a guest account, include the DRISHTI account ID if you can still see it in Settings.
  3. Support will explain how to verify ownership before processing the request. Keep your app installed if you still need it to identify your account. Never email passwords, sign-in codes, payment-card details or copies of your readings.

Email a deletion request

Delete from the app

In a version that offers account deletion, open Settings → Account & purchase recovery → Delete account and app data. Follow the confirmation and sign-in prompts. Account deletion is permanent.

What deletion covers

Deletion covers the DRISHTI account and associated data held by DRISHTI, including its profile, private Library backups, reading recovery records and uploads, and account-linked wallet data. The in-app flow also clears DRISHTI data from that installation.

We may retain limited purchase, fraud-prevention and safety records for as long as needed to meet legal or accounting obligations, resolve disputes, prevent duplicate grants or protect other users.

If you have an active subscription, manage or cancel it separately in Google Play. Deleting your DRISHTI account does not cancel store billing.
